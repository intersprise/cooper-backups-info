# Privacy Policy — Cooper Backups

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Cooper Backups is a personal-use application maintained by
the GitHub user intersprise. It is not a public service.

## Google Drive access

The application uses Google Drive access to create, read,
verify and manage encrypted backups in the owner's account.
Deleting outdated backups is part of the retention policy.

Google account passwords are not collected by the application.
Authorization is handled by Google through OAuth.

## Storage and security

Backup contents are encrypted by restic before being uploaded.
Google Drive stores the encrypted backup files.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on the owner's devices.
They are not published in this repository.

## Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for
advertising. Backup contents are not intentionally shared
with other users. Google processes stored files according
to its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

## Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account settings.
Revoking access stops future authorized operations but does
not automatically delete existing backups.

The owner can delete backup files through Google Drive.
